Australian Medicines AU — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 September 2026 · Applies to: Australian Medicines AU version 1.0.7

Overview

Australian Medicines AU is designed as an offline medicine-reference application. This release does not require a user account and does not intentionally collect personal or sensitive user data.

Information collected

This release does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, tracking, or social-media SDKs. It is designed not to require Android internet permission.

Search and app-use data

Medicine and brand search terms are processed locally on the device. Australian Medicines AU does not send search terms or normal app-use activity to a developer-controlled server and does not maintain a developer-held search history.

Data sharing

Australian Medicines AU does not sell or share personal or sensitive user data with advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers, or other third parties because this release does not collect that data. Android, Google Play, the device manufacturer, or other system services may process technical information independently under their own terms and privacy policies.

Storage, retention and deletion

The medicines database bundled with the app is not user data. This release does not create a user account and does not retain personal data on a developer-controlled system. Any temporary app state remains on the device. Uninstalling the app removes app-managed local data subject to normal Android backup, restore, and operating-system behaviour. There is no developer-held account or personal-data record to request deletion from in this version.

Security

Because this release does not transmit personal or sensitive user data to the developer, there is no developer-operated transmission or cloud storage of such data. Local app files are protected by normal Android application sandbox and device security controls.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. It has no account, messaging, social, profiling, or targeted-advertising functionality.

Future changes

If a future release adds internet connectivity, accounts, analytics, advertising, subscriptions, cloud services, crash reporting, or another feature that changes data handling, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will need to be updated before that release is published.

Developer and privacy enquiries

App: Australian Medicines AU. Privacy enquiries can be submitted using the developer contact email displayed on the Australian Medicines AU Google Play Store listing. The developer should keep that contact information current.

Educational / informational limitation

Australian Medicines AU is a reference aid for education and information only. It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for professional assessment, diagnosis, treatment, prescribing, dispensing judgement, or emergency care. Verify current official TGA/PBS and relevant product information when making medicine-related decisions.
